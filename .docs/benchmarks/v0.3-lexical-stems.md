# v0.3 lexical stems (step 51)

Plan: `.docs/plans/2026-10-13-orag-v0.3-lexical-stems-plan.md`. On the rewritten v0.2 set
(`v0.3-set-rewrite.md`) with the 0.3.0-alpha.3 engine, hybrid misses 9 of 89 answerable
dev questions (in context 0.899). This step first measures where each missed label is,
then tests adding a short prefix stem of each long query word to the lexical query.

## Why questions miss (dev)

`orag eval retrieval --rank-depth 50` (default models, 2026-10-14) reports each label's
rank in the lexical, dense and hybrid lists down to 50. Every metric is unchanged by the
depth (pinned by `deeper_ranks_change_no_metric`); hybrid in context stays 0.909 overall.
Only dev rows were read. For every dev question whose evidence misses the 8-chunk answer
context, the missing label (index within the question's labels) and its ranks
(`–`: not in the top 50):

| Question | Category | Label | Hybrid | Lexical | Dense | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v2-fac-031 | factual | 0 | 10 | 16 | 6 | near |
| v2-xl-011 | cross_language | 0 | 28 | – | 16 | deep |
| v2-xl-021 | cross_language | 0 | 24 | – | 9 | near |
| v2-md-012 | multi_document | 1 | 13 | 21 | 9 | near |
| v2-md-018 | multi_document | 0 | 31 | 38 | 35 | deep |
| v2-id-023 | identifier | 0 | 20 | 28 | 49 | deep |
| v2-acc-019 | accentless | 0 | 9 | 12 | 9 | near |
| v2-inf-001 | inflection | 0 | 10 | 13 | 12 | deep |
| v2-inf-013 | inflection | 0 | – | – | – | absent |

- **absent** (1): neither list returns the label in its top 50. Fusion cannot reach it.
- **deep** (4): a list has it, but no list ranks it within 10. Fusion can only lift it by
  pushing other evidence out; better recall in one list (stems for the lexical list) can.
- **near** (4): a list ranks it within 10 and fusion drops it from the context. This is
  step 50's ground (context diversity, rejected); these rows went to orag-e7.

This corrects the earlier, coarser reading (step 50 and the plan): the multi-document
misses are not all absent. `v2-md-018`'s `Dinlenmek, çalışanların hakkıdır` is at lexical
38 and dense 35, present but far too deep; `v2-md-012`'s pipe table is near. Stems target
the lexical side of the deep and absent rows; the decision below counts every category.

## Decision rule (written on 2026-10-14, before any variant is scored with real models)

It is not changed after the numbers are in. A lexical-only feasibility probe on the dev
split (plan `.docs/plans/2026-10-13-orag-v0.3-lexical-stems-plan.md`) came first; holdout
was not read.

Variants: `prefix:3+stem:4`, `prefix:3+stem:5`, `prefix:3+stem:6`, `prefix:3+stem:7`, each
against the served `prefix:3`, with `orag eval retrieval --lexical-query <variant>` on
`eval/corpus/v02` + `v02-retrieval.jsonl`, default models, hybrid strategy, paired per
question on "in context". The variant with the highest dev wins minus losses is chosen
(ties: the larger stem, which adds fewer terms). It replaces `prefix:3` only if:

1. wins minus losses ≥ 1 on **dev** (owner: even a small real gain is taken; conditions 2–5
   guard against a gain that is noise or a trade);
2. wins minus losses ≥ 0 on **holdout** (read once, for the chosen variant);
3. no category loses more than 1 question net (dev and holdout together);
4. factual, identifier and table questions together lose nothing net (dev and holdout
   together): the gain must not come from trading short single-fact questions;
5. `seed.jsonl`, `anayasa-tr.jsonl` and `anayasa-cekim-tr.jsonl` lose at most 1 question
   net each on hybrid in context;
6. hybrid p95 latency on the v02 set rises by at most 5 ms;
7. it stays query side: no reindex, no `LEXICAL_VERSION` bump, no model call.

Otherwise `prefix:3` stays and the result is recorded here.

## Results (2026-10-14, 0.3.0-alpha.3 engine, default models)

Dev, hybrid in context, paired per question against `prefix:3`:

| Variant | W−L | Wins | Losses | Hybrid p95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `prefix:3` (served) | – | – | – | 13.9 ms |
| `prefix:3+stem:4` | +1 | v2-acc-019, v2-inf-001 | v2-md-001 | 14.1 ms |
| `prefix:3+stem:5` | 0 | v2-inf-001 | v2-md-001 | 13.9 ms |
| `prefix:3+stem:6` | −1 | – | v2-md-001 | 14.2 ms |
| `prefix:3+stem:7` | **+2** | v2-id-023, v2-inf-013 | – | 15.0 ms |

The effect is not monotonic in the stem length: shorter stems add more terms that match
broadly and push `v2-md-001`'s evidence out, while `stem:7` adds only the stems of words of
eight or more characters. The lexical-only probe in the plan pointed at `stem:5`; with
dense and fusion in the loop the longer stem is the better one.

`prefix:3+stem:7` is chosen (highest dev W−L). The rule, condition by condition:

| # | Condition | Result | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | dev W−L ≥ 1 | +2 (2 wins, 0 losses) | yes |
| 2 | holdout W−L ≥ 0, read once | +1 (v2-inf-012), 0 losses | yes |
| 3 | no category loses more than 1 net | inflection +2, identifier +1, every other 0 | yes |
| 4 | factual + identifier + table lose nothing net | +1 | yes |
| 5 | guard sets lose at most 1 each | seed 0, anayasa-tr 0, anayasa-cekim-tr +3 (cek-19, cek-24, cek-30) | yes |
| 6 | hybrid p95 rises at most 5 ms | +1.1 ms | yes |
| 7 | query side only | no reindex, no `LEXICAL_VERSION` bump, no model call | yes |

**Adopted.** Over the whole v02 set, hybrid in context 0.909 → 0.926, recall@10 0.931 →
0.949, MRR@10 0.673 → 0.691. The multi-document misses are unchanged: the stem `dinlenm`
lifts `v2-md-018`'s `Dinlenmek` passage from lexical rank 38 to 10, but dense still misses
it and fusion keeps it out of the context.

## Answers (reported, outside the rule)

`orag eval answers --sampler dry` on `v02-answers.jsonl` (40 answerable, 20 unanswerable),
both runs on the same engine, only the lexical mode differs:

| Mode | Expected facts | Cited evidence | Valid citations | Abstention P / R | p50 ms | p95 ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `prefix:3` | 0.925 | 0.925 | 1.000 | 0.842 / 0.800 | 1313 | 2879 |
| `prefix:3+stem:7` | 0.850 | 0.925 | 1.000 | 0.842 / 0.800 | 1292 | 3021 |

Per probe, six change:

- `v2-ans-037` (holdout, "fiyat teklifinin geçerliliği kaç gün"): the stem `geçerli` also
  matches the price list's validity dates, the "15 gün" chunk leaves the context and the
  model abstains. A loss caused by retrieval.
- `v2-ans-011` (holdout) and `v2-ans-016` (dev): the gold evidence is still in the context and
  cited; the answer is shorter and omits an expected fact (for `v2-ans-016` the `60 ay` of the
  export terms). Generation variance under a different context.
- `v2-ans-027` (holdout): no longer abstains, cites the evidence (fact count unchanged).
- `v2-ans-047` (unanswerable): now abstains. `v2-ans-041` (unanswerable): answers "not
  explicitly stated … set out in a separate document", a hedged refusal that the scorer
  counts as an answer. Abstention P/R is unchanged overall.

Expected facts fall by 3 probes (one from retrieval, two from generation with the evidence
present); cited evidence and abstention do not move.

## Confirmation on 0.3.0-alpha.4 (step 52 headings merged first)

Step 52 changed chunk boundaries, so the comparison was re-run on top of it as a
confirmation: the variant stays `prefix:3+stem:7`, chosen on dev above; this is a second
holdout read on a changed baseline, not a tuning round.

Retrieval, hybrid in context, paired against `prefix:3`: dev +1 (v2-id-023), holdout +1
(v2-inf-019), no losses, no category loses, factual + identifier + table +1; seed 0,
`anayasa-tr` 0, `anayasa-cekim-tr` +1 (cek-19); p95 13.9 → 14.3 ms. Over the whole set
0.920 → 0.931, recall@10 0.926 → 0.931, MRR@10 0.689 → 0.710. The rule still passes.

Answers, sampler `dry`, each mode run twice (the two runs of a mode gave identical answers):

| Mode | Expected facts | Cited evidence | Valid citations | Abstention P / R | p95 ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `prefix:3` | 0.875 | 0.975 | 1.000 | 0.941 / 0.800 | 2879 |
| `prefix:3+stem:7` | 0.925 | 0.975 | 1.000 | 0.941 / 0.800 | 3545 |

On this engine the sign is reversed against alpha.3. Per probe: `v2-ans-032` now answers
with the cited 3.500 TL limit instead of abstaining (a real gain); `v2-ans-011` moves the
other way from alpha.3 and both of its scores are scorer artefacts (it expects only
`iş günü`, so "10 iş gündür" misses and a refusal that quotes the passage matches; found
by orag-e7 in step 52); among unanswerable probes two more refuse and two fewer, abstention
unchanged. Generation is deterministic but sensitive to which chunks fill the context, so
the answer set shows no reliable effect of stems in either direction at this size; the
retrieval gain holds on both engines.
