# v0.2 model decision

## Baseline (Task 42, 2026-10-12)

The v0.2 set (`eval/corpus/v02`, `v02-retrieval.jsonl`: 175 answerable and 25
unanswerable questions; `v02-answers.jsonl`: 40 answerable and 20 unanswerable
probes) with the default models, before any retrieval change. The set is
model-authored and model-reviewed, not human-checked (D-018).

- Machine: Apple M5 Max, macOS (Metal)
- Version: `orag 0.2.0-alpha.16` with the Task 42 harness (release build)
- Embedding model: `qwen3-embedding-0.6b-q8_0`; generation model: `qwen3.5-4b-q4_k_m`
- Commands: `orag eval retrieval --corpus eval/corpus/v02 --dataset eval/datasets/v02-retrieval.jsonl`
  and `orag eval answers ... --dataset eval/datasets/v02-answers.jsonl --sampler dry`

### What the baseline shows

- **Fusion loses cross-language questions.** Dense retrieval alone puts the
  evidence of 22 of 25 cross-language questions in the answer context (0.880);
  hybrid only 15 (0.600). The lexical list finds nothing useful when the
  question and the document share no words (0.280), yet its top hits take
  context slots and its leader is promoted to the front. This is the largest
  gap in the set and the first thing a fusion change has to fix.
- Hybrid misses 20 questions in context; 12 of them dense alone finds (v2-fac-011, v2-md-006, v2-md-016, v2-md-017, v2-tab-015, v2-xl-001, v2-xl-005, v2-xl-018, v2-xl-021, v2-xl-022, v2-xl-023, v2-xl-025), and dense misses 15 that hybrid finds (v2-acc-006, v2-acc-012, v2-fac-003, v2-fac-017, v2-fac-028, v2-fac-035, v2-fac-039, v2-id-006, v2-id-009, v2-id-011, v2-id-014, v2-md-008, v2-md-013, v2-md-020, v2-tab-007). Both lists matter; how they are fused decides.
- Identifiers and tables are found well (0.920, 0.960 in context); their MRR
  (0.770, 0.748) leaves room for ranking, not for recall.
- Accentless questions are all found in context (1.000), because the dense side
  reads them; lexically 0.950.
- Holdout trails dev (hybrid in context 0.862 vs 0.909). Changes are tuned on
  `dev` and adopted only when `holdout` does not drop (D-018).
- Answers: 34 of 40 answerable probes complete, 5 refused although answerable,
  17 of 20 unanswerable ones refused.
