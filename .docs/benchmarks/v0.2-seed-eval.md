## Regression gate against v0.1.0 (Task 46 Step 2): failed, waived

`ORAG_MODEL_DIR=… scripts/compare-v01.sh` builds the `v0.1.0` tag and the
candidate and scores both with the same embedder on the same corpora. The
predeclared gate: hybrid recall@10 and MRR@10 of the candidate at least those
of v0.1.0, on `seed.jsonl` and on the text-only subset of the v02 set.

Per question on seed, rank of the evidence chunk in hybrid retrieval:

| question | v0.1.0 | candidate |
|---|---|---|
| tr-001 … tr-006, en-001 … en-005, x-003 (12) | 1 | 1 |

**The gate failed** on seed MRR@10 by 0.036, which is exactly one question
moving from rank 1 to rank 2 (0.5 / 14). It is **waived for v0.2.0**: x-002
stays in the answer context, its dense rank already flipped between 1 and 2
across Metal and CPU in an earlier release check (a near-tie, so the same
binary can pass or fail this gate depending on the backend), and the
90-question subset, the only sample large enough to show a change, improves
on every metric. The waiver was decided by the orchestrating agent under the
owner's standing delegation of release decisions to the advisors
(2026-10-10), on fable-5.1's recommendation (gpt-6-astra was out of quota);
the owner can revoke it.

The rule stays as written for this release. For the next one it is reformed
(plan, Task 46 note): on seed, gate recall@10 and in-context only (yes/no
metrics suit 14 questions); gate MRR@10 only on sets of at least 90 questions;
and add a paired criterion on questions lost from the answer context. The
cross-language fusion fix is planned for v0.3 on the 200-question dev /
holdout protocol.
