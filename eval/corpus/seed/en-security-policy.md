# Information Security Policy

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must be rotated every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access.

## Incident Response

Security incidents must be reported to the security team within 4 hours of discovery. The incident hotline is extension 4410.

## Data Retention

Audit logs are retained for 400 days. Customer support transcripts are deleted after 90 days.
