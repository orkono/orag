# Deniz Sulama Sistemleri A.Ş. — Export Warranty Terms

## 1. Scope

These terms cover drip irrigation products listed in the Deniz Sulama Drip Irrigation Product Catalogue for the fourth quarter of 2026 and identified by product codes beginning with DS-. The catalogue is the reference for product descriptions, technical ratings and prices; these terms do not change any technical rating.

Only the products named in section 2 carry a manufacturer's warranty. Any product not named in section 2 is sold without a manufacturer's warranty, and the statutory rights of the buyer under the law of the buyer's country remain unaffected.

These terms apply between Deniz Sulama and its direct customer (the importer or distributor). End users must address warranty claims to the distributor from whom they purchased the product.

## 3. What the warranty covers

Deniz Sulama warrants that the products named in section 2 are free from defects in material and workmanship under normal agricultural use for the stated period.

If a covered defect is confirmed, Deniz Sulama will, at its own choice, repair the product, replace it with the same or an equivalent product, or issue a credit note for the invoiced value of the defective quantity.

Replacement products carry only the remaining warranty period of the original product; no new warranty period begins with a replacement.

The warranty does not cover the cost of removal, reinstallation, labour, or lost water, nor loss of crop or yield.

## 4. Exclusions

The warranty does not apply to defects or damage resulting from:

- operation above the maximum pressure stated for the product in the catalogue;
- ultraviolet degradation of products that are not marked as UV-stabilised in the catalogue;
- damage caused by rodents, insects, birds, farm machinery or cultivation tools;
- injection of acids, chlorine or fertilisers outside the dosage guidance in the catalogue maintenance section, including continuous chlorine dosing above 10 ppm;
- freezing of water inside the product;
- consumables, namely batteries, disc sets, screens, gaskets, diaphragms and solenoid coils;
- products from which the batch number or serial label has been removed or made illegible;
- modification or repair by anyone other than Deniz Sulama or a service partner authorised in writing.

Normal wear, discolouration and cosmetic marks that do not affect function are not defects.

## 5. Claim procedure

A warranty claim must be submitted within 30 days of the discovery of the defect. Claims submitted later are rejected regardless of the remaining warranty period.

Claims are submitted on warranty claim form WF-01, which is available from the export portal, and are sent by e-mail to export@denizsulama.example.

The claim must state the invoice number, the product code, the batch number printed on the product, the quantity affected and a description of the failure, and must include at least three photographs showing the defect and the installation.

For drip pipes and tapes the customer must retain a sample of at least 1 metre of the defective product for inspection. Deniz Sulama may request that the sample be shipped to İzmir, and in that case pays the shipping cost.

For claims whose invoiced value exceeds 5,000 EUR, Deniz Sulama may send a technician to inspect the installation on site before deciding the claim.

Defective products must not be destroyed or returned without a written return authorisation from the export department.

## 6. Response times

Deniz Sulama acknowledges receipt of a claim within 2 business days by e-mail, quoting a claim reference number.

A decision on the claim is communicated within 15 business days of receiving the complete documentation listed in section 5. If an on-site inspection is required, this period starts after the inspection.

Replacement goods for an accepted claim are dispatched within 30 calendar days of the acceptance, delivered FOB İzmir in the same way as a normal export order.

A customer who disagrees with a decision may appeal in writing to the export manager within 10 business days; the appeal is answered within a further 10 business days and the answer is final.

## 7. Limitation of liability

The liability of Deniz Sulama under these terms is limited to the invoiced value of the defective products. Deniz Sulama is not liable for indirect or consequential loss of any kind, including loss of crop, loss of profit or costs of alternative irrigation.

Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot be excluded under applicable law.
